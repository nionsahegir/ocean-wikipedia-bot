# 🌊 Ocean Wikipedia (@oceanwikipedia) — Curation & Publishing Playbook

## 1. Core Curation Rules
1. **Niche Authenticity:** 100% pure marine biology, ocean phenomena, underwater exploration, deep-sea creatures, and ocean conservation. Zero unrelated footage or generic clips.
2. **Proper Attribution / Creator Credit:**
   - Every curated post MUST prominently credit the original creator:
     `🎥 Video by / Credit: @creator_handle`
     `📸 Photo by: @photographer_handle`
   - Tag the creator in the caption.
3. **2026 Hashtag Strategy:**
   - Modern, niche-specific tags (mix of broad, mid-tier, and community):
     `#OceanWikipedia #MarineBiology #DeepSea #OceanLife #UnderwaterWorld #OceanConservation #SeaLife2026 #OceanLovers`
4. **Caption Formula:**
   - Line 1: Hook / Curiosity Fact (3 seconds)
   - Body: 2-3 short, punchy paragraphs explaining the science or behavior
   - Question: Open-ended call-to-action question to provoke comments
   - Attribution: Polite, respectful credit tag
   - Hashtags: 8-12 targeted tags
